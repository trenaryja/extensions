Round 5's flaw is fixed by rule, not by eye: the glyph pair's bounding circle must stay 4 units inside the inner ring — pairs that would collide are scaled down automatically (fixed fit), or the rings grow to hug the pair (hug fit). Colors are exclusively Tailwind v4 tokens in oklch: light ground is -100 / -950 / -400 (disc / ink / ring), dark is -950 / -50 / -600. The font field is wide open — every face below ships with macOS; grayed cards aren't installed on this machine. Flip guides on to see the safe circle. Nothing here is decided.
