### What's inside the playground

- **Tables** you edit like a spreadsheet — click a cell, drag a column to reorder.
- **Callouts** — note, tip, warning, quote… foldable and nestable.
- **Math** — inline `$…$` and block `$$…$$`, rendered as you type.
- **Diagrams** — Mermaid flowcharts, sequence diagrams, and more.
- **Code** — syntax-highlighted to match your VS Code theme.

Everything is live and editable. It's a scratch copy, so nothing you do there touches your projects.
